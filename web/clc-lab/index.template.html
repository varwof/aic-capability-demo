What is evaluated

Authorization, capability entailment, parameter bounds, intersection, containment, obligation resolution, constraint union, chain authorization and decision records.

What this page is not

A signature verifier or a carrier implementation. CLC judges authority semantics; certificate status, signatures and transport are separate layers.

Reproduce elsewhere

The vector files are copied from varwof/capability. Evidence JSON can be downloaded and compared with the Go or Python runners.
